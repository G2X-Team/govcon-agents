# Incumbent research

**Use for:** incumbent, competitor or partner research backed by awards.

Resolve legal entity and identifiers before joining records. Keep parent companies, subsidiaries, joint ventures, award recipients and subcontractors distinct. Define the question and period. Use award history to establish documented work, not current relationship quality or willingness to team.

Capture award ID, recipient, awarding/funding office, description, dates, vehicle, obligation measure and source. Distinguish initial and modified dates and values when the source supports them. Identify related orders without double counting parent vehicles and orders.

**Prompt:** Build an incumbent brief for this named entity and contract. Separate what the records establish from our competitive hypotheses. Include relevant scope and dates, office/vehicle context, source links and five questions to investigate. Do not infer customer satisfaction or privileged relationships.

**Return:** an evidence ledger, a short assessment and a diligence list. Missing data does not establish no awards, no subcontracting or no relationship. Record the retrieval date and limitations of the source coverage.
