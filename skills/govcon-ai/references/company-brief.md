# Company brief

**Use for:** company context, ideal pursuit profile and search constraints.

Ask for the capabilities and deliverables the company can actually provide, examples of relevant past work, target buyers, NAICS/PSC starting points, geography, capacity, desired prime/sub role and hard exclusions. Record certifications and vehicle access only with evidence and a verification date. Mark unknowns; do not infer eligibility from a company name or marketing description.

Create a one-page brief with sections: identity; capability; proof; target; constraints; exclusions; unknowns; source/date. Distinguish a saved company record from temporary instructions for today's research. For a third-party company, never change the user's own calibration defaults.

**Prompt:** Use the company material I supplied to draft a reusable company brief. Separate documented capabilities and qualifications from statements needing evidence. Ask me to confirm the target market before searching. Do not edit a saved profile.

**Return:** brief, missing proof and the specific filters it can support. A fictional example stays labeled fictional; never fabricate a UEI, certification or past performance.
