# Agency account plan

**Use for:** market selection and account-based government sales.

Define the offering, buyer type, time period, geographic scope and evidence sources. Separate the department, component, program, awarding office and funding office. Buying activity at one office does not establish demand everywhere in its parent department.

Research relevant award history, mission priorities, public forecasts, current notices, incumbent patterns and accessible vehicles. Keep obligations, ceilings, potential values and contract counts separate. Never add a first page and label it total spend. Use supported aggregates for totals or return an explicitly bounded sample.

Build an account table: office; relevant mission; evidence of buying this work; incumbent/vehicle context; entry route to investigate; unknowns; next research action. Rank by the user's stated criteria with reasons, not an unsupported probability of winning.

**Prompt:** Compare three candidate buying offices for our stated offering. Give a sourced reason to prioritize each, a reason to disqualify or defer it, and one question that would change our decision. Keep facts and sales hypotheses separate.

**Return:** a short target list and one actionable account plan. Do not invent buyer relationships, personal contact details, budgets or meetings.
