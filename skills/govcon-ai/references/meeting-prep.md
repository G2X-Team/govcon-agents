# Meeting and industry-day preparation

**Use for:** discovery meetings, partner discussions and public industry days.

Confirm the event, participants, purpose, date/time zone and desired outcome. Verify official registration and public-event details from the controlling source. A related event is an alternative, not an exact match. Respect acquisition-specific communication instructions.

Prepare a one-page brief: mission; relevant buying/award evidence; what the company can substantiate; three hypotheses; five answerable questions; what not to assume; desired next step. Separate facts about the office from facts about individual attendees. Do not invent contact details or personal relationships.

**Prompt:** Prepare me for this meeting using the supplied agenda and public evidence. Make the questions specific to the buyer's stated mission and our capabilities. Identify claims I need to verify before saying them. Draft a follow-up structure without sending or scheduling anything.

**Return:** brief and question list. After the meeting, use actual notes to draft follow-up and proposed CRM updates. Do not report promises, attendance or interactions that did not occur.
