# Pipeline decisions and follow-up

**Use for:** a weekly pursuit review or a stale-opportunity cleanup draft.

Read the permitted pipeline export or supported current records. Confirm the review date, stage definitions, owners and management criteria. Check changed deadlines, evidence of activity and unresolved next steps. Distinguish missing activity records from proof no work occurred.

Group decisions as advance, investigate, hold or stop, with evidence and a named next action. A high stated value does not imply a high probability or expected revenue. Use only the user's approved probability model if one exists; otherwise omit weighted revenue.

**Prompt:** Review this pipeline as of the stated date. For each pursuit, give the decision needed, supporting evidence, blocker, owner and next action with a proposed due date. Separate factual corrections from management recommendations. Produce proposed changes only.

**Return:** decision table and an exact change preview. Do not delete items, change stages or send reminders unless specifically authorized. Before an authorized save, resolve record IDs, preserve unrelated values and read back. Never log a planned call as a completed interaction.
