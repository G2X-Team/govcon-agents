# Recompete watch

**Use for:** potential follow-on work and forward capture planning.

Define the capability, office, date window and contract scope. Retrieve awards approaching recorded end dates and check their latest modification and option context where available. Find possible follow-on notices using identifiers, buyer and scope. Treat similarity as a lead.

Use three distinct labels: confirmed follow-on relationship supported by a source; possible relationship needing verification; expiring award with no follow-on found in the sources checked. A recorded end date is not a solicitation date, guaranteed recompete or proof of current status.

**Prompt:** Make a potential recompete watch for the stated period. Preserve award IDs and end dates. Put confirmed follow-on notices in a separate column from possible matches. For every uncertain candidate, give the source we should check next and a suggested review date. Do not turn on monitoring unless requested and supported.

**Return:** award; incumbent; scope; office; recorded dates; related notice; evidence label; unknown; next check. A suggested watch is a draft; a recurring watch exists only after the host actually creates it and reads it back.
