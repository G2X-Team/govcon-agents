# Teaming shortlist

**Use for:** finding complementary prime or subcontract partners.

Start with the gap: technical capability, past performance, customer knowledge, contract vehicle, capacity or documented eligibility. Resolve company identities and relevant public award evidence. Keep marketing claims separate from verified records and current certifications. Use the user's exclusions.

Assess complementarity, not resemblance alone. Identify overlaps, conflicts and unknown willingness to team. Do not claim exclusive access, consent, a relationship or a commercial agreement.

**Prompt:** Identify up to five potential partners for this exact capability gap. For each, show relevant evidence, why the combination might work, a potential conflict and questions to resolve before outreach. Draft a specific discussion agenda, but do not send messages or create contacts.

**Return:** gap; company/entity ID; evidence; complementary role; conflict; diligence questions; next decision. Keep outreach drafts separate from sent outreach, and planned conversations separate from completed CRM interactions.
