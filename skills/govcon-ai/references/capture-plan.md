# Capture plan and decision memo

**Use for:** deciding whether and how to pursue a specific opportunity.

Use the confirmed company brief, complete available requirements, buyer/mission evidence, incumbent context, route to market, team capacity and known deadlines. Identify hard eligibility conflicts before speculative competitive analysis. Unknowns belong in a validation plan.

Create sections: opportunity and customer need; fit evidence; gaps; discriminators to prove; likely competitors with evidence; team/vehicle route; actions and owners; milestones; resource assumptions; decision. Distinguish a management recommendation from an authorization to bid or spend.

**Prompt:** Draft a capture plan and a pursue, hold or stop recommendation. Tie each reason to evidence. For a conditional recommendation, specify the missing fact, owner, deadline and consequence if unresolved. Do not invent win probability, customer preference, partner commitments or pricing.

**Return:** decision memo, evidence table and action plan. Use the user's existing evaluation framework when provided. Do not send outreach, assign work in an external system or launch paid analysis merely because an action is recommended.
